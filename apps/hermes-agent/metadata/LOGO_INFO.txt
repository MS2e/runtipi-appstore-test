Hermes Agent Logo
================

This is a custom-created logo for the Hermes Agent RunTipi app.

Design elements:
- Dark blue background (#1a1a2e)
- Glowing blue accents (#4a9eff) 
- Bot head with antenna (representing AI agent)
- Wings on both sides (Hermes = messenger god with winged sandals)
- Concentric rings (symbolizing "grows with you")

The logo represents Hermes as a self-improving AI agent that:
- Has "wings" for speed and autonomy
- Features a bot head for AI capabilities  
- Shows growth rings for the learning/improvement aspect
